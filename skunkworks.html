The Core Technology

At the heart of this work is an ultra-rugged, fault-tolerant edge-triggering platform built around a triple-redundant 2-out-of-3 voting system. It utilizes three 6-axis IMUs, culminating in 18 distinct data points, all sampled at 1,600 times a second. This architecture eliminates single-point sensor dropouts, prevents high-g saturation glitches, and makes deterministic, split-second safety and actuation decisions locally at the edge.

While this platform originated from a personal hardware project focused on fall mitigation (because I’m tired of falling, breaking bones, and dealing with longer recovery times than when I was much younger), its underlying capabilities open up massive potential across multiple critical industries:

  • Aerospace & Flight Test Instrumentation: Modernizing how test articles capture high-g, multi-axis data without legacy mechanical failure points.
  • Autonomous Systems & Robotics (AGVs/Cobots): Providing fail-operational safety layers that eliminate single-point sensor dropouts and false-trigger risks.
  • Heavy Industrial Machinery & Structural Health Monitoring: Tracking harmonic distortion, vibration thresholds, and dynamic load shifts on massive infrastructure.
